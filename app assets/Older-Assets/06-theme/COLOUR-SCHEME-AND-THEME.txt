VISUAL LANGUAGE
---------------
- Ancient Egyptian / forbidden temple / desert oasis.
- Near-black obsidian canvas with restrained bronze-gold borders.
- Red is used for Locked, danger, sacred jewel highlights and emphasis.
- Water/flexible state uses muted blue-cyan rather than bright Web3 neon.
- Thin 1px borders, square/low-radius cards, engraved-frame feel.
- Low-opacity radial glows rather than bright glassmorphism.
- Gold should look aged and metallic, not yellow.
- Use realistic oasis/pharaoh art as atmospheric hero art; UI must remain readable.
- Desktop remains wide and cinematic, but follows the same component hierarchy as mobile.
- Mobile uses single-column vault-first flow and persistent bottom navigation.

NOTES ON THIS ASSET PACK
------------------------
1. The supplied concept images are flattened AI-generated design mockups, not layered design source files.
2. Cropped logos, icons, pharaoh art and backgrounds in this pack are extracted from those generated mockups.
3. Files named "*-transparent-approx.png" use an automated near-black background removal and may require edge cleanup for production.
4. The textures are newly generated helper assets based on the established Forbidden Oasis palette; they are not claimed as original website source layers.
5. The original website HTML primarily uses CSS, inline SVG/data textures and rendered Three.js rather than a directory of reusable raster artwork.
