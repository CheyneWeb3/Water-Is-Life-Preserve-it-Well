# WATER Dividend-Preserving Staking

## Stake Without Giving Up Your BNB Dividends

Traditional staking can create a problem for dividend-paying tokens.

When tokens are transferred into a normal staking contract, the staking contract becomes the on-chain holder. This can mean the staking contract receives the token's dividend rewards instead of the individual user.

WATER Staking is designed differently.

Instead of combining everybody's tokens inside one staking wallet, **each user receives their own dedicated on-chain staking vault**.

Your vault holds your WATER while you are staked and remains eligible to receive the normal BNB rewards generated by WATER.

At the same time, the staked WATER earns additional WATER from the staking reward pool.

This means staking does not replace your existing rewards.

It adds another reward layer.

# Hold WATER

Holding WATER normally allows your wallet to participate in the token's existing BNB dividend system.

### You receive

**BNB dividends**

Your WATER remains inside your connected wallet and participates normally in the WATER ecosystem.

---

# Stake WATER

When you stake WATER, the platform creates your own permanent staking vault.

Your WATER moves from your wallet into your vault.

The vault then becomes the on-chain holder of your staked WATER.

### Your staked position can earn

**BNB dividends**

plus

**WATER staking rewards**

Your BNB dividend system continues operating while your WATER simultaneously participates in staking.

---

# Your Personal Staking Vault

Every user receives their own unique smart-contract vault.

Think of it as your personal on-chain staking account.

```text
YOUR WALLET
     │
     │ Stake WATER
     ▼
YOUR STAKING VAULT
     │
     ├── Holds your WATER
     │
     ├── Receives WATER's BNB dividends
     │
     └── Accumulates WATER staking rewards
```

Your vault is created for you the first time you stake.

It can then be reused whenever you stake again.

There is no need to create another vault every time you enter or leave staking.

# Two Separate Reward Streams

WATER Staking keeps the two reward systems separate.

## BNB Dividends

These come from WATER's existing dividend mechanism.

Your staking vault holds your WATER and participates in the dividend system as an on-chain holder.

BNB earned by your vault belongs to you.

You can claim it to your wallet.

---

## WATER Staking Rewards

These are additional rewards supplied specifically for staking.

A dedicated allocation of WATER is placed into the staking reward system.

Rewards are released over time and distributed between active stakers according to their share of the staking pool.

For example:

```text
Daily WATER staking rewards: 100,000 WATER

User A owns 80% of the active stake
User B owns 20% of the active stake

User A earns 80,000 WATER
User B earns 20,000 WATER
```

The rewards come from the funded staking allocation.

They are **not taken from other users' deposits**.

# Flexible Staking

Flexible staking is designed for users who want access to their WATER without committing to a fixed period.

### Flexible staking provides

**BNB dividends**

*

**Standard WATER staking rewards**

*

**Withdraw when available under the flexible staking rules**

There is no long-term commitment required.

---

# Locked Staking

Locked staking can provide increased WATER reward weight in exchange for committing your WATER for a selected period.

Possible lock options may include:

```text
30 Days

90 Days

180 Days

365 Days
```

Longer locks can receive greater staking weight.

For example:

```text
Flexible      1.00x reward weight

30 Days       1.10x

90 Days       1.25x

180 Days      1.50x

365 Days      2.00x
```

Final lock periods and reward weights are determined by the active staking program.

During the lock period, the WATER remains inside your personal staking vault.

Your vault can still continue earning its eligible BNB dividends and WATER staking rewards.

---

# How Staking Rewards Are Calculated

The staking system releases a defined amount of WATER over time.

Your share depends on how much of the active staking pool belongs to you.

For example:

```text
Total staked
10,000,000 WATER

You stake
1,000,000 WATER

Your pool share
10%
```

If the pool releases:

```text
50,000 WATER
```

during that period, your proportional reward would be:

```text
5,000 WATER
```

before any applicable lock multiplier or weighting.

Rewards continuously accumulate according to the staking rules.

The system does not need to manually send rewards to every wallet every day.

Your earned rewards are calculated automatically by the staking contracts and become available when you interact with your position.

---

# A Finite Reward Pool

WATER staking rewards are backed by WATER allocated specifically for staking.

For example, if 20% of the WATER supply is reserved for staking, the allocation could be separated into:

```text
10% Flexible Staking Rewards

10% Locked Staking Rewards
```

Each pool can then release its allocation over a predetermined period.

This makes the reward system transparent.

Users can see:

* the total staking allocation;
* remaining WATER rewards;
* current emission rate;
* total WATER staked;
* their percentage of the pool;
* rewards already earned; and
* estimated current yield.

The staking pool does not depend on new users depositing funds to pay existing users.

# Why Individual Vaults?

Most staking systems place every user's assets into one large staking contract.

WATER Staking separates each user's position.

```text
STAKING CONTROLLER
        │
        ├──── User A Vault
        │
        ├──── User B Vault
        │
        ├──── User C Vault
        │
        └──── User D Vault
```

The staking controller manages the overall staking program and reward calculations.

The individual vaults hold the users' WATER and receive their corresponding rewards.

This architecture allows WATER's existing dividend system and the new staking system to operate together rather than forcing users to choose between them.
